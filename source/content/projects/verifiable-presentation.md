The specification and
[minimal reference implementation](https://github.com/verifiable-presentation/impl)
for a plugin-based service that allows issuers to render verifiable
presentations, based on the
[W3C verifiable credential specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model)
from templates, and store it in a queryable database for holders to
list/retrieve.
