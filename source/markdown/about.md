## About Me

I am an undergraduate computer science student at
[IIIT Hyderabad](https://www.iiit.ac.in). I have been writing open
source code since 2018, and have contributed across 400+ issues and pull
requests in 30+ repositories on GitHub. In 2024, I worked with MIT Media
Lab's Large Population Models (LPM) team on their
[AgentTorch](#agent-torch) project for differentiable agent based
learning, and in 2025, I worked with Google DeepMind to
[extend and benchmark function calling in the Gemma 3 family of models](/projects/offline-function-calling)
as part of Google Summer of Code. In 2026, I am working with
[Davide Baldelli](https://dundalia.github.io) on
[Building a Robust Activation Monitor](https://sparai.org/projects/f26/recEOHVc40u0mglPU),
a [SPAR](https://sparai.org) fellowship project on activation monitors
that stay reliable across models, concepts, and contexts. My current
areas of interest are mechanistic interpretability and diffusion models.

I co-maintain the popular node.js libraries
[express-rate-limit](#express-rate-limit) and
[express-slow-down](#express-slow-down), used to shield servers from
DDOS attacks, with their original author
[Nathan Friedly](https://github.com/nfriedly). I am also a contributor
to [serve](#serve), [Refined Github](#refined-github), [FPM](#fpm),
[Beckn](#beckn), [Sunbird Registries and Credentials](#sunbird-rc), and
have created projects such as
[Dabbu](/projects/#dabbu-knowledge-platform) (a knowledge platform),
[office-text-extractor](/projects/#office-text-extractor), as well as
[yeet](/projects/#yeet) (a package manager for Arch Linux), amongst
others. I co-created the [Mess Portal](https://mess.iiit.ac.in) and
associated systems at IIIT Hyderabad with
[Aarnav Pai](https://github.com/arnu515), and continue to maintain them.
I participated in the national
[ONDC Grand Hackathon](/projects/#ondc-grand-hackathon) in 2022 and my
team won a special mention award. I was also invited to give a talk
titled 'A 12 Grader's Journey to Opensource Contribution via Sunbird and
Beckn' at the
[Sunbird DPG Tech Fusion Conference, Pune](https://lu.ma/kinhufit),
in 2024. I also gave a talk on my work on AgentTorch at the
[FOSS United Meetup, Hyderabad](https://fossunited.org/c/hyderabad/hyd-2024-aug-archive-fbf7d1ca),
in 2024.
